# Assets

All art is **generated by code** in this repository (`tools/assets/`, Blender via the `bpy` module + numpy textures).
Nothing was downloaded, nothing is copied from any game, and no real-world weapon model is reproduced: the weapons
(AR-7 rifle, P-9 pistol, combat knife) are fictional designs loosely inspired by familiar silhouettes (wood-and-steel rifle, heavy pistol, tanto knife). Style target: semi-realistic low-poly with PBR
materials (concrete, painted/corrugated steel, wood, burlap, nylon, polymer, gunmetal, rubber, leather).

### What each model contains

* **Soldier**: helmet (shell, rails, NVG mount, ear guards, rear skirt), goggles, face gaiter, plate carrier with plates/pouches/pack, belt and cargo pockets, knee/elbow/shoulder pads, gloves, boots with soles; separate head/neck/torso/arm/leg parts bound rigidly to a 19-bone armature; team colour on shirt, trousers, helmet stripe and arm patch.
* **Rifle**: stamped receiver with dust cover, wooden-look hand guards, stock and grip (polymer with a brown tint), gas tube over the barrel, post sight, slant muzzle brake, steel banana magazine (own node), charging handle (own node), ejection port, selector, mag catch.
* **Pistol**: heavy serrated slide with top rib and barrel hood (own node, kicks back when firing), sights, frame with rail slots, trigger + guard, textured grip panels, base plate, slide stop, magazine (own node).
* **Knife**: tanto blade with flat grind and fuller, brass cross-guard and pommel, ridged rubber grip, lanyard tab.
* **First-person variants**: the same weapon plus gloved hands in a grip pose (right hand on the grip, left hand cupping the handguard / wrapping the pistol grip, fist around the knife) and sleeves.
* **Map**: one prop per collision box in `client/shared/map.json` (so visuals equal collision): bevelled concrete barriers with panel joints, plinth/cap and lamps; sandbag walls (running-bond bags); corrugated steel containers (3 paint variants) and braced wooden crates with steel corner brackets; perimeter walls with hazard band, pilasters, steel doors and lamps; tiled concrete floor with joints.

### Crossroads map

`python tools/assets/gen_crossroads_map.py` writes `client/shared/map_crossroads.json` (collision boxes: perimeter, four corner buildings with doors/windows, no vehicles or street obstacles) and `crossroads_kit.py` builds `map_crossroads.glb` from exactly those boxes. Original layout, original textures (asphalt, brick); the name/idea is generic "crossroads", no third-party map data or assets. Previews: `docs/previews/crossroads_*.jpg`.

### Piazza, Pyramid, Canyon, Harbor, Bunker maps

Five more maps, original layouts that are only inspired by well-known archetypes (village with a square, desert ruin around a stepped pyramid, canyon with a long mid corridor, container yard, indoor close-quarters complex); generic names, no copied geometry, textures, names or logos, no third-party assets. Each is written by `tools/assets/gen_<id>_map.py` (pure python, deterministic) to `client/shared/map_<id>.json` and built by `tools/assets/<id>_kit.py` (Blender) into `client/assets/models/map_<id>.glb`:

Every box gets its own prop, so visuals equal collision; only wall decor (door/window frames, facade windows, stall awnings) is visual-only. New numpy textures in `texgen.py`: `stucco`, `terracotta`, `sandstone`, `sand`, `rock`, `cobble` (existing ones unchanged). Previews: `docs/previews/<id>_overview.jpg` (Cycles), in-game shots `docs/screenshots/maps_<id>_overview.jpg` / `_street.jpg` (`e2e/maps.mjs`).

### Decals and pickups (no new files)

Bullet holes, blood splats/pools/spray are generated at runtime (canvas textures, `client/js/decals.js`). Pickups reuse the weapon glbs (`client/js/pickups.js`).

## Rebuilding

See `tools/assets/README.md`. `blender -b -P tools/assets/build_all.py` (or `python tools/assets/build_all.py` with `pip install bpy==5.2.2`) regenerates every `.glb` and the preview renders in `docs/previews/`; add `--no-preview` to skip renders.
`python tools/assets/report.py` prints the table above. Previews: `docs/previews/`, in-game screenshots: `docs/screenshots/` (`E2E_QUALITY=high node e2e/screenshots.mjs`).

## Rendering

PBR (`MeshStandardMaterial` from glTF), ACES filmic tone mapping, sRGB output, procedural sky → PMREM environment for reflections, hemisphere + directional sun with a 2048² PCF-soft shadow map (characters and props cast and receive), separate first-person pass so weapons never clip into walls. In-game option *Graphics: Low* turns shadows off and uses pixel ratio 1.
